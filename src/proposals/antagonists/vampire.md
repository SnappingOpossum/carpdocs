## Overview
the idea is for a new minor antag of a vampire being on the station, draining blood from crew members while trying to live. Part of the idea is tht they are not hostile against the crew but are however still a blood sucking parasite on board so their survial comes at the cost of potentially harming the crew. 
## Gameplay
The idea is the vamp is going to need to feed every so often on the crew, repeated feeding crippling the crew member, but a lack of feeding cripples the vamp. the vamp has 4 hunger states, wellfed, normal, underfed, fiending. to do this they have the ability to mesmerize crew, mesmerizing a crew member inflicts force sleep, flash immunity gives you immunity to mesmerize. next is the feed ability which only deals minor pierce damage and drains blood from the target. the wellfed state is just that they dont need to feed for a while and look normal, normal is a state where the vamp looks like a normal member of the crew but they may need to feed soon, underfeed is a state where the vamp looks sickly but isnt dying, fiending is a state where the vamp is actively taking damage and will require blood in order to not die, the vamp is clear as day as to what they are. to propperly feed the vamp require living humanoids, feeding on non-humanoid, dead humanoids and blood backs keeps them out of fiending but not much more than that. blood from rotten corpses will deal damage to the vamp.

feeding from humanoids will inflict a status, the status worses if the vamp has overfed, if it reaches the final status the only way to cure it is to kill the vamp. but thats not the only reason to kill the vamps their dead body can either to get a good chem catalyst or the ability for those with more selfish intentions to harvest the vamps organs for personal use, though this renders the chems useless.
