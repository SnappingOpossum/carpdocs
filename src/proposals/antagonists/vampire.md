## Overview
the idea is for a new minor antag of a vampire being on the station, draining blood from crew members while trying to live. Part of the idea is tht they are not hostile against the crew but are however still a blood sucking parasite on board so their survial comes at the cost of potentially harming the crew. 
## Gameplay
The idea is the vamp is going to need to feed every so often on the crew, repeated feeding crippling the crew member, but a lack of feeding cripples the vamp. the vamp has 4 hunger states, wellfed, normal, underfed, fiending. to do this they have the ability to mesmerize crew, mesmerizing a crew member inflicts force sleep, flash immunity gives you immunity to mesmerize. next is the feed ability which only deals minor pierce damage and darins blood from the target.
