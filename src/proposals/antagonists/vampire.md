## Overview
The idea is for a new minor antag of a vampire being on the station, draining blood from crew members while trying to live. Part of the idea is that they are not hostile against the crew but are however still a blood sucking parasite on board so their survial comes at the cost of potentially harming the crew.

## Gameplay
The idea is the vamp is going to need to feed every so often on the crew, repeated feeding crippling the crew member, and a lack of feeding cripples and slowly kills the vampire. The vampire has 4 hunger states, wellfed, normal, underfed, fiending. While wellfed, the vampire's natural abilities are improved and they look like a normal crew member, though there is a cap to how much blood can be stockpiled so that they require continuous feeding to keep themselves in this state. The normal is a state where the vampire starts off, giving them access to all their regular abilities and appearing the same as a normal crew member. Underfeed is a state where the vamp looks sickly but isn't actively dying yet. Fiending is last chance of a vampire before they die. They slowly take damage over time in this state and will require any source blood very soon. The vampire is clear as day as to what they are.

To do this they have the ability to mesmerize crew, mesmerizing a crew member inflicts forced sleep, flash immunity gives you immunity to mesmerize. Next is the feed ability which only deals minor pierce damage and drains blood from the target. To propperly feed the vampire requires living or recently living humanoids, feeding on non-humanoid, humanoid corpes past fresh, and blood backs keeps them out of fiending and can stave off their hunger but no more than that. Blood from rotten corpses will deal damage to the vamp.

Feeding from humanoids will inflict a status, the status worses if the vamp has overfed, if it reaches the final status the only way to cure it is to kill the vamp. But thats not the only reason to kill the vamps their dead body can either to get a good chem catalyst or the ability for those with more selfish intentions to harvest the vamps organs for personal use, though this renders the chems useless.
